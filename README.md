# Leonida Trophy Atlas

An offline GTA 6 trophy guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies: every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a guide in our own words with cited sources, a five-stage roadmap to the platinum, missable flags, and a checklist whose progress is saved in the browser (with export and import).
